This is the part disk maps skip. For every big folder, Roomy tells you what it is, who it belongs to, what happens if you delete it, and whether it comes back on its own.

Then it labels it SAFE REVIEW or KEEP, and points to the better way to clean it when there is one. Click a folder to try it. These are the real results from that 85%-full Mac.

Mac storage questions, answered

What people search for when their Mac says the disk is almost full.

What is taking up space on my Mac?

Usually things you never see: app caches in ~/Library/Caches, Xcode build data and simulators, node_modules folders, Docker and virtual machine disks, local AI models, old iPhone backups and duplicate downloads. Roomy scans your home folder in seconds and lists the biggest ones first, with an explanation for each.

Is it safe to delete ~/Library/Caches on a Mac?

The contents, yes: apps rebuild their caches as needed, though some may be slower the first time. The folder itself should stay, so Roomy keeps the folder and moves what's inside to the Trash. Quit apps first to clear more.

How do I free up space from Xcode?

DerivedData and iOS DeviceSupport are safe to delete: Xcode rebuilds or re-copies them. Simulator devices and archives are worth a look first. Running xcrun simctl delete unavailable removes simulators for iOS versions you no longer have.

Can I delete node_modules, Docker images or Ollama models?

node_modules comes back with npm install. For Docker, don't delete its disk file; run docker system prune -a instead. For Ollama, ollama rm removes one model at a time. Roomy shows the right command for each.

How is Roomy different from DaisyDisk, GrandPerspective or CleanMyMac?

Disk maps show you what's big. Roomy also tells you what each folder is, what happens if you delete it, and whether it comes back on its own. It's free, open source, runs offline, and only removes things when you click.

Does Roomy upload my files?

No. Roomy has no network code at all. It reads file names and sizes on your Mac and nothing leaves it.
